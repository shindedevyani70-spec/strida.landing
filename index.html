Built to make you a more consistent runner

Most people don't quit running because it's hard. They quit because they lose the habit. Strida is designed around keeping it.

  • Know when to push and when to rest

    Your recovery score combines sleep, resting heart rate and training load, so a hard day never turns into an injury.

  • Light enough to forget you're wearing it

    At 32 g with a soft silicone strap, it's comfortable on a 5 km jog and a 5 am alarm alike.

  • Works with the apps you already use

    Syncs your runs to Strava, Google Fit and Apple Health. Android and iPhone both supported.

  • Your data stays yours

    Health data is encrypted on the watch and never sold. Export or delete it any time.
